Our Story

Born from a love of light and fragrance

AK Royal Flames began with a simple idea: a candle should do more than light a room — it should set a mood, carry a memory and make a space feel like home.

Every candle is hand-poured in small batches with premium wax and carefully balanced fragrances, then finished with designs that look as beautiful unlit as they do glowing.

Our Mission

Small joys, thoughtfully made

Our mission is to create candles that are beautiful, long-lasting and made with care — perfect for gifting, celebrations and quiet evenings alike — while keeping ordering simple and personal through WhatsApp.
